Auto-zoom

Every click, in focus.

Demodrop watches where you click, zooms in on the moment that matters, then eases back out. There's nothing to keyframe.

  • AutomaticEvery click becomes a smooth zoom, with no setup.
  • EditableDrag, resize or delete any zoom block, or add your own.
  • 1.2× to 3×Choose how close each zoom gets.
Cursor

A cursor that looks filmed.

Demodrop records without the pointer, then the editor draws a crisp, smoothed one, so it stays sharp at any zoom.

  • SmoothedShaky movement becomes a steady glide.
  • Click ripplesA soft ring marks every click so viewers can follow along.
  • Hides when idleIt steps out of view when you stop moving, at whatever size you choose.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? New versions and notes are on GitHub Releases.

Is Demodrop really free?

Yes. There's no account, no trial, no watermark and no paid tier. Download it and use it.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Recording, editing and exporting all happen on your computer. Your videos are saved as normal files on your disk, and you decide where they go.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognize it. Click More info → Run anyway to continue. Signing is planned.

Does it work on a Mac?

There's a macOS preview build, but it hasn't been tested on real hardware yet. Windows is the main platform for now.

What can I export?

MP4 or GIF, in 16:9, 1:1 or 9:16. MP4 is best for posting on X, and GIF works well in READMEs, docs and chats.
