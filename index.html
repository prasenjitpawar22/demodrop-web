How it works

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Demodrop really free?

Yes. There's no account, no trial, no watermark and no paid tier. Download it and use it.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Recording, editing and exporting all happen on your computer. Your videos are saved as normal files on your disk, and you decide where they go.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognize it. Click More info → Run anyway to continue. Signing is planned.

Does it work on a Mac?

There's a macOS preview build, but it hasn't been tested on real hardware yet. Windows is the main platform for now, and the Mac version will improve.

What formats can I export?

MP4 or GIF, in 16:9, 1:1 or 9:16. MP4 is best for posting on X; GIF works well in READMEs, docs and chat.

Who makes Demodrop?

Demodrop is built by Prasenjit Pawar. New versions are published on GitHub Releases.
