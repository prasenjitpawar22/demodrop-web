Private by design

Your recordings never leave your computer.

Recording, editing and exporting all happen on your machine. There’s no account to create and no server for your videos to go to.

  • No accountOpen it and start recording.
  • No cloud, no uploadsNothing you record is sent anywhere.
  • No telemetryNo analytics, no tracking, no usage pings.
  • Plain filesSaved to Videos\Demodrop. Yours to keep.

The only network request Demodrop makes is checking GitHub for a new version.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? New versions and notes are on GitHub Releases.

Is Demodrop really free?

Yes. There’s no account, no trial, no watermark and no paid tier. Download it and use it.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Recording, editing and exporting all happen on your computer. Your videos are saved as normal files on your disk, and you decide where they go.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Only the direct installer warns. Get Demodrop from the Microsoft Store and it installs without a warning and keeps itself up to date. The installer from GitHub isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn’t recognize it: click More info → Run anyway to continue.

What can I export?

MP4 or GIF, in 16:9, 1:1 or 9:16. MP4 is best for posting on X, and GIF works well in READMEs, docs and chats.
