Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Demodrop records your screen on your computer and keeps everything there. There's no account, no analytics and no uploads. The developer never receives your recordings or anything about how you use the app.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Demodrop desktop app for Windows and macOS, wherever you installed it from (the Microsoft Store, the download on this site, or GitHub Releases), and this website. Demodrop is made by Prasenjit Pawar, an independent developer.

What Demodrop records

Only while you're recording, and only the source you picked:

  • The screen or window you chose, as a video.
  • The pointer: where it is, its shape, and when you click, so the editor can zoom and draw a smooth cursor.
  • That a typing key was pressed, and when, so typing can be sped up. Demodrop never logs which key, so nothing you type can be read back from a recording.
  • Your microphone and your computer's sound, only if you turn them on for that recording.

Where it's kept

Each recording is a folder of plain files on your computer, in Videos\Demodrop on Windows and Movies/Demodrop on macOS. Your edits are saved beside it, and exports go wherever you save them. None of it is sent anywhere. Delete a recording in the app (it goes to the Recycle Bin or Trash) or delete its folder, and it's gone.

Demodrop also keeps a few app settings on your computer, such as saved styles. Uninstalling removes the app. Your recordings stay until you delete them.

What goes over the network

  • Microsoft Store version: Demodrop makes no network requests. The Store handles updates under Microsoft's own privacy statement.
  • Direct download (this site or GitHub): when it starts, and every few hours while it's open, Demodrop asks GitHub Releases whether there's a newer version, and downloads it if there is. That request carries nothing about you or your recordings. GitHub sees the usual details of any web request, such as your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement.

There's no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Permissions

Windows or macOS will ask before Demodrop can record the screen (macOS) or use the microphone. You can take these back at any time in your system's privacy settings. Demodrop keeps working without the microphone.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, and it's hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Downloads come from GitHub.

Children

Demodrop collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Since Demodrop collects nothing, a change could only be about something new, such as a new update channel.
